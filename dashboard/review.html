What each shot means

Drive (D)
Any hard or medium-pace ball hit after the bounce (includes what used to be "groundstroke" - how hard it was comes from the measured speed).
Drop (P)
Soft ball that falls into the kitchen, hit from the back or middle of the court.
Dink (K)
Soft ball into the kitchen, hit from the kitchen line (both teams usually at the net).
Reset (T)
Soft block of a fast incoming ball that takes the pace off, usually defensive.
Lob (L)
High ball over the opponent's head.
Overhead (O)
Smash hit from above the head.
Not a hit (N)
Nothing was hit here: a missed swing, the ball hitting the net, picking the ball up after the rally, a bounce. These teach the hit detector.
Wrong player / marker (W)
A real shot, but the dashed box is on the wrong player or the yellow ring is not on the ball.
Unclear (U)
Can't tell, wrong player marked, or not a real hit - use it freely; unclear answers are not used for training.

The dashed box is the player the system thinks hit; the yellow ring marks the ball at the moment of contact. Volley = hit before the ball bounced (can be any shot type).
