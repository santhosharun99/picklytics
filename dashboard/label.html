Add the bounces

  1. Click a rally in the list on the right (start with ones that show 0 bounces). Your hits are already there.
  2. Play it (Space, - to slow down). Every time the ball touches the ground, pause and press X.
  3. Ball landed outside the court lines? Press U right after X.
  4. Wrong one? Click it in the list, press Backspace.
  5. Done with the rally? Press Enter. The Bounces column gets a ✓.

Every rally has at least 2: the serve and the return must bounce. Ball into the net and dropping: no need to mark. A frame or two off is fine (← → steps one frame).

Just mark the hits

  1. Click a rally on the right list. Press Space to play. Press - to slow down.
  2. Every time a player hits the ball, pause and press
    1 if it's the player at the bottom of the screen
    2 if it's the player at the top of the screen
  3. A hit in the list that didn't happen? Click it, press Backspace.
  4. Rally finished? Press Enter. Next rally.

No bounces, no shot names. Being a frame or two off is fine. Use ← → to step one frame if you want to be exact.

Keys

Spaceplay / pause ← →1 frame back / forward (Shift: 1 s, Alt: 5 s) - =slower / faster (0.25× to 1×) 1hit by NEAR player (bottom of screen) 2hit by FAR player (top of screen) Xbounce Umark the selected bounce as out (landed outside the court) clickon the ball: set its position for the selected event (optional) S R D P Kserve, return, drive, drop, dink L O T Glob, overhead, reset, groundstroke F Bforehand / backhand Vtoggle volley (hit before the bounce) [ ]start / end rally (rallies also start on the first mark) Wswap ends (who is on the near side) Entermark rally reviewed (auto-labelled rallies only count once reviewed) Backspacedelete selected event Zundo

Mark a hit at the frame the paddle touches the ball; mark a bounce at the frame the ball touches the court. Serve and return are filled in automatically. Third shots are derived later, so just label them drop, drive, etc.

Shot guide

SServe: first shot, underhand, diagonal RReturn: second shot, after the bounce KDink: soft tap near the net that drops into the kitchen PDrop: soft arc from the back of the court into the kitchen DDrive: hard, fast, flat LLob: high, over the opponent's head OOverhead: smash from above the head TReset: soft block that takes the pace off a hard shot GGroundstroke: normal hit from the back, not soft, not a drive VVolley: hit before the ball bounced (add to any shot)

Kitchen = the 7 ft zone each side of the net. Not sure? Leave the shot blank; hits and bounces matter most.
